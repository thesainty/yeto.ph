```text

[ ] Publish a private or selected-friend update

Did you need help? Where?

Something easy or useful:

Something confusing or broken:
1. I opened…
2. I tapped/entered…
3. I expected…
4. Instead…

Could you reproduce it? Always / Sometimes / Once
Did it stop you finishing the task? Yes / No

Did the post audience and plan invitations make sense?
Did you understand that the map/journey tools are demos?

One thing you would improve first:
Would you use TARA again with your people? Why or why not?
```
